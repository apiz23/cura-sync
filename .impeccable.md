## Design Context

### Users
Final year university project for a healthcare platform (CuraSync). Dual audience: (1) general public / patients finding clinics, booking appointments, tracking medications; (2) academic reviewers (professors/examiners) assessing the project. Both must be served simultaneously — credibility matters as much as usability.

### Brand Personality
Precise, institutional, trusted. CuraSync should feel like proper medical software that belongs in a clinical environment — not a startup landing page. Reliability and clarity without coldness or intimidation.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light interface (dark mode respected). Strong typographic hierarchy using Libre Franklin for display headings — editorial, institutional heritage, not on the reflex list. Green primary (oklch 0.72 0.17 155 / dark: 0.79 0.16 153) used sparingly as a trust signal, not decoration. Geometric precision elements preferred over soft blobs. Both light and dark themes active.

### Design Principles
1. Typography leads — hierarchy through scale and weight, not decoration
2. Precision over warmth — clean lines, purposeful spacing, nothing vague
3. Green accent is earned — used sparingly so it signals trust, not noise
4. Content earns its place — every section answers "what can the user do next?"
5. Confident layout — asymmetry and varied spacing over safe centered grids
